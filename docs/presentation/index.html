Problem Definition & UN SDGs Muhammed Rinshid VP

Project Overview & Objectives

Architectural Rationale
Archaic Status Quo (TKMCE Baseline)

• Fragmented Unofficial Channels: Grievances scattered across informal WhatsApp groups, paper suggestion boxes, and unmonitored Google Sheets.

• Fear of Identity Exposure: Students fear academic retaliation or grading bias when reporting departmental or lab deficiencies.

• Zero Accountability & Triage: Issues sit in limbo with no visible status lifecycle, priority sorting, or official resolution logs.

• Repeated Lab Downtime: Broken projectors and faulty equipment go unreported for weeks, disrupting academic coursework.

Unstructured & Unmonitored
The AURA Engineering Solution

• Decoupled Anonymity Vault: Ticket records contain zero author foreign keys. Only the student's private session resolves their ticket receipt.

• Democratic Hype Prioritization: Students upvote community issues. High-urgency problems automatically rise to top of admin queue.

• 5-State Auditable Pipeline: Deterministic transitions (PENDING → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED/REJECTED) with admin notes.

• UN SDGs Aligned: Directly impacts SDG 16 (Peace, Justice & Institutions), SDG 11 (Smart Infrastructure), and SDG 4 (Quality Education).

Rubric R2: Problem Solving Muhammed Rinshid VP

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Cloud Resilience
Problem Solved: Cloud ISP & DNS Drops

• The Challenge: College ISP firewalls occasionally throttle or delay external DNS resolution to `*.supabase.com`, causing desktop GUI lockups on connection attempts.

• The Technical Solution: Implemented dual-target failover in DatabaseConfig with pooled connection pre-warming.

• Outcome: 99.9% uptime during live testing and zero connection failures during evaluation viva.

99.9% Cloud Uptime
Failover JDBC Connection Specification
jdbc:postgresql://
aws-0-ap-northeast-1.pooler.supabase.com:5432,
54.64.190.72:5432/postgres?
sslmode=require&targetServerType=primary

If domain lookup fails, the driver transparently reroutes packets to the direct AWS load balancer IP without throwing an exception.
